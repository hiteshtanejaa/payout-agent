# Contractor Payout Policy (FIN-POL-007, version 2026-10)

Owner: Finance Lead. Applies to all contractor payments made by the Payout Agent.

## Clause P1 — Approved payees only
Payments may only be made to contractors on the approved contractor register with an active contract. Any invoice from an unknown payee is rejected.

## Clause P2 — Auto-approval limit
Invoices up to £1,000.00 may be paid autonomously by the Payout Agent. Invoices above £1,000.00 require explicit approval from the Finance Lead before payment.

## Clause P3 — Monthly contract cap
Total payments to a contractor in a calendar month must not exceed the monthly cap stated in that contractor's contract. Invoices that would breach the cap are rejected.

## Clause P4 — Rate match
The invoiced amount must equal the hours billed multiplied by the contracted hourly rate, within a 1% tolerance. Mismatches are held for review.

## Clause P5 — No duplicate payments
An invoice number may only be paid once per contractor. Duplicates are rejected.

## Clause P6 — Daily spend ceiling
Total autonomous payouts across all contractors must not exceed £3,000.00 per day. Invoices that would breach the ceiling are held.

## Clause P7 — Open disputes and finance notes
If finance records an open dispute, hold, or concern about a contractor, the payment is held for human review until the note is resolved.
